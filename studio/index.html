HOW IT WORKS

Build a route, not a subscription.

Generate: Move the satellite map to an area, choose distance, and select Generate. You can regenerate alternatives.

Draw: Choose Draw waypoints and click the map. Drag markers to reshape, right-click to remove, or use Undo and Reverse. Routes snap to mapped walking paths; manual off-road mode is available.

Strict gravel/dirt: Build from connected trail geometry carrying OSM gravel/dirt tags or individually confirmed surfaces. If a continuous full-length route cannot be assembled, the app shows a shortfall instead of inserting paved roads.

Surface trainer: Inspect high-resolution NAIP crops around trails and teach a browser-local color/texture classifier by labeling examples. Predictions remain estimates; manual review is required before strict routing uses a previously unknown segment. Google Maps imagery is not used for model training.

Discover small trails: Reveal even unnamed paths with missing surface tags (yellow dashed). Click a path and choose Add to route to keep its shape as a manually reviewed segment.

Aerial Scout: Search NAIP aerial photos for possible thin paths. This experimental feature can mistake roofs, drainage, and field lines for trails. Review imagery and access before using it.

COROS: Download or share the GPX. Open it with the COROS app on your phone, save to your route library, then sync to your watch.

This is an independent route planner, not affiliated with Sherpa Map. Public services have fair-use limits, and unknown surfaces remain unknown.
